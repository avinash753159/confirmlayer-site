Why ConfirmLayer

Contract managers want a record. Whoever runs your systems wants something simple to set up. Here is what both usually ask first.

Why would a government subcontractor verify identity?

Public contracts often put workers on government sites, in government buildings, or in front of the public. The prime and the agency expect you to know who those workers are. ConfirmLayer gives you a consistent way to check each person before they start.

What exactly do you check?

That the government ID is authentic and has not been altered, that the selfie matches the portrait on the ID, and that a live person is in front of the camera.

How does a worker complete it?

On their own phone. They photograph their ID and take a selfie through a hosted link you send them, or through your own onboarding screens if you use the API.

What record do we keep?

Each check produces a timestamped result with the extracted fields and the reason it passed, failed or went to review. You decide whether results are stored with us or sent to your systems and not retained.

A timestamped result with the fields and the reasons, kept or not kept as you choose.

Scope

What It Is, and What It Is Not

ConfirmLayer confirms that an ID document is genuine and that the person presenting it is the person on it. It is not a background check, a security clearance, or employment eligibility verification, and it does not query government identity databases.

Ask If It Fits Your Contract
  • The ID is authentic and has not been altered
  • The selfie matches the portrait on the ID
  • The person presenting it is live
  • No background or criminal record checks
  • No clearance or employment eligibility checks
  • No government database lookups

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers on scope, documents and data. For anything else, email us.

What is ConfirmLayer?

An identity verification service for government subcontractors. It checks that a worker's government ID is genuine and that the person presenting it matches the photo on it.

Which documents do you cover?

We are US-first: United States driver's licenses, state ID cards and the US passport. If you need another document, email us and we will tell you whether we can add it.

Is this a background check or a clearance?

No. We verify the document and the person presenting it. We do not run criminal record checks, security clearances or employment eligibility verification, and we do not query DMV, passport or other government databases.

If your contract requires any of those, you will need them in addition to ConfirmLayer.

Do we need developers to use it?

No. The hosted link needs no development work. If you have an onboarding system already, the API connects to it.

How do you handle ID images and personal data?

Images are processed by our verification pipeline, which includes cloud-based OCR sub-processors. You choose whether results are stored with us or sent to your systems and not retained. We do not sell verification data. See the privacy summary, which is still marked as a draft.

How do we get started?

Email hello@confirmlayer.com with a short description of the contract and how many workers you need to verify. We reply with next steps.
