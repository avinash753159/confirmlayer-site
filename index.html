“What if someone just looks older?”

We don't guess age from a face.

Looking 25 doesn't get anyone through. Neither does looking young when you're 30. The age comes from the ID, not the camera.

The ID proves the age.

We read the date of birth on a government photo ID and check that the ID itself is real.

The selfie proves it's their ID.

The selfie is matched to the photo on the ID, and checked to be a live person, not a photo of a photo.

Questions.

What if my customer looks older or younger than they are?+

It doesn't matter. We never estimate age from a face. The age comes from the date of birth on their ID. The selfie is only used to confirm the ID belongs to the person ordering.

Does every customer have to do this?+

Only customers buying age-restricted products. We set up which orders need a check with you.

Do I need a developer?+

No. We add the verification step to your Shopify checkout and set it up with you on a 15-minute call.

What happens when a check fails?+

The order is held in a simple review queue, so you decide what to do with it.

What do I keep on file?+

A timestamped record of every check that you can pull anytime.

Who handles my customers' ID data?+

Verification runs on certified third-party infrastructure, and customer data is collected with the customer's explicit consent.
