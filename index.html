Questions.

Will this hurt my sales?+

The check only triggers on orders with age-restricted items, so the rest of your store is untouched. It's built for phones, where most of your customers already are. And a failed check goes to your review queue, not to an automatic cancellation.

Do repeat customers verify every time?+

That's up to you. We'll configure how often verification triggers on the setup call, based on what you sell and your risk tolerance.

Which IDs do you accept?+

US driver's licenses and other government-issued photo IDs, plus passports. The ID has to be real and unexpired, and the selfie has to match the photo on it.

What does the 60-day pilot cost?+

The same as the monthly plan you pick: $79/mo on Starter, $249/mo on Growth, $749/mo on Scale. Run it on your real store for 60 days. If you stay, it's credited toward your annual plan.

What happens when a check fails?+

The order stays held in a simple review queue, so you decide what to do with it, including whether to refund.

Do I need a developer?+

No. We connect it to your store and set it up with you on a 15-minute call.

What if my customer looks older or younger than they are?+

It doesn't matter. We never estimate age from a face. The age comes from the date of birth on their ID. The selfie is only used to confirm the ID belongs to the person ordering.

What do I keep on file?+

A timestamped record of every check that you can pull anytime.

Who handles my customers' ID data?+

Verification runs on certified third-party infrastructure, and customer data is collected with the customer's explicit consent.

Is there a long contract?+

You start with a 60-day paid pilot, credited toward your annual plan. No minimums to start, and early-access pricing is locked for 12 months.
