How it works

From a photo to a verdict in five steps.

Every scan follows the same pipeline. No black boxes: you see the extracted fields, the confidence behind each one, and exactly why a check passed or failed.

01

Capture

Your user snaps a photo of their ID, plus an optional selfie. Works on any phone camera.

02

Identify

We match the document's layout against our template library to identify the exact ID type and version.

03

Authenticate

Layout, fonts, security features, and data consistency are checked against the expected format for tampering.

04

Extract

Text is read from the visual zone, MRZ, or barcode and returned as clean, structured JSON.

05

Decide

You set the rules. We return accept, review, or reject, with per-field confidence scores.

What we verify

  • The document is authentic and unaltered
  • The face in the selfie matches the ID photo
  • The person is live, not a photo of a photo
  • Every field extracted with a confidence score

What we don't do

  • We don't query government or DMV databases
  • We can't confirm an address exists in official records
  • If you need record-backed identity proofing, that's a different product, and we'll tell you so

FAQ

Asked and answered.

Do you check government databases?

No. We verify that the document is authentic and unaltered, and that the person presenting it matches the photo and is live. We don't query DMV, passport, or other government records, and we won't pretend otherwise.

If your compliance regime requires record-backed identity proofing, that's a different product category. We'll tell you that upfront instead of selling you something that doesn't qualify.

How is this different from Stripe Identity or Persona?

Two things: price and friction. Enterprise IDV tools charge $0.80 to $1.89 per check and route you through a sales process. Our checks work out to $0.059 to $0.089 per credit, you sign up with any email, and you get 100 free credits to test everything before paying anything.

What exactly costs a credit?

One ID scan costs one credit. Adding face match costs one more credit. Adding liveness costs one more. A full check with everything enabled is three credits, and you choose which features each verification uses.

How long does integration take?

Most developers verify their first ID within ten minutes of getting an API key. The hosted verification flow, a prebuilt page that handles capture, liveness, and the selfie, needs no frontend work at all: just send your user a link.

Which IDs do you support?

At launch: every US state driver's license and the US passport. New documents ship every release cycle. If you need a specific document, email us and we'll prioritize it.

Do you store ID images?

Your choice. Keep a transaction vault with us for audit and review, or disable storage and have results sent straight to your own systems. Either way, you control retention.

Can I resell this to my customers?

Yes. The API is white-label friendly with no ConfirmLayer branding, and volume pricing is built to leave margin for resellers. If you're building onboarding, compliance, or marketplace software, we'd like to be your invisible backend.

What happens when a document isn't recognized?

You get a clear warning, not a silent failure: the document is flagged as unrecognized and your rules decide whether that's a review or a reject. Send us the document and we'll typically add support within a day.

Do I need a sales call to start?

No. Sign up with any email, take your 100 free credits, read the docs, and integrate. We have a support team, not a sales team.
