Why ConfirmLayer

The prime wants proof. Your office wants something that does not add work. Here is what each side asks first.

Why verify identity at all?

Public work puts your people inside government buildings, on government sites and in front of the public. When someone asks who was on the job, a name on a timesheet is a weak answer. A verified ID, matched to the person who showed up, is a strong one.

What does a check actually confirm?

Three things: the ID is authentic and unaltered, the selfie matches the portrait on it, and a live person is in front of the camera. You also get the fields read from the document, such as name, date of birth and expiry date.

How much work is it for the crew?

Two photos on their own phone: the ID and a selfie. You send a link, they follow it. Nothing to install and no trip to the office.

What do we have to show for it afterward?

A timestamped result for each worker, with the fields read from the ID and the reason the check passed, failed or went to review. You choose whether we store it or pass it to your systems and keep nothing.

What It Does

Six pieces of work you would otherwise do by hand, or not do at all.

Document Reading

Name, date of birth, ID number and expiry date, read off the card so nobody retypes them.

Face Match

The selfie is compared to the portrait on the ID. Same person, or not.

Liveness

Confirms a real person is on camera, not a printout or a photo on another screen.

Altered-ID Detection

Layout, fonts and data are tested against what that document is supposed to look like.

Decision Rules

You set the bar. Every check comes back as accept, review or reject.

A Record of Each Check

A timestamped result per worker, with the reasons, stored or not stored as you choose.

Scope

What It Does, and Where It Stops

ConfirmLayer answers one question well: is this ID real, and is this the person on it? It is not a background check, a security clearance or employment eligibility verification, and it does not query government databases. If your contract calls for those, you still need them.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers on scope, documents and data.

What is ConfirmLayer?

An identity verification service for government subcontractors. It checks that a worker's government ID is genuine and that the person presenting it matches the photo on it.

Which documents do you cover?

We are US-first: United States driver's licenses, state ID cards and the US passport. More documents are added over time.

Is this a background check or a clearance?

No. We verify the document and the person presenting it. We do not run criminal record checks, security clearances or employment eligibility verification, and we do not query DMV, passport or other government databases.

If your contract requires any of those, you will need them in addition to ConfirmLayer.

Do we need developers to use it?

No. The hosted link needs no development work. If you have an onboarding system already, the API connects to it.

How do you handle ID images and personal data?

Images are processed by our verification pipeline, which includes cloud-based OCR sub-processors. You choose whether results are stored with us or sent to your systems and not retained. We do not sell verification data. See the privacy summary, which is still marked as a draft.
