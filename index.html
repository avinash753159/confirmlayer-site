How It Works

From Upload to Verdict in Three Steps

Every verification follows the same path. End to end in seconds, no human in the loop, structured JSON back to your application.

01

Capture

The user photographs their ID and, if you ask for it, takes a selfie. Use your own screens with a direct REST call, or our hosted page.

02

Verify

We identify the document, test it for tampering, read its fields, and compare the selfie to the portrait, all in one round trip.

03

Decide

Your thresholds turn the result into accept, review or reject. The response carries the fields and the reasons.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers on scope, coverage, pricing and data. Anything missing, email us and a founder replies.

What is an ID verification API?

It is a service your software calls to check an identity document. You send an image of the ID, and optionally a selfie, and get back the data on the document, a judgment on whether it is genuine, and whether the person presenting it matches the photo.

It replaces the work of building document reading, tamper checks and face comparison yourself.

Which documents do you cover?

We are US-first: United States driver's licenses, state ID cards and the US passport. We do not publish a country or document count we cannot back.

If you need a document outside that set, use the request-a-document link and we will tell you whether and when we can add it. Email us for the current coverage list before you build.

Do you check government databases?

No. We verify that the document is authentic and unaltered, and that the presenter matches it and is live. We do not query DMV, passport or other government records.

If your compliance regime requires record-backed identity proofing, that is a different product category and we will tell you so upfront.

How does the free tier work?

Email hello@confirmlayer.com from any address and ask for a key. You get 100 test credits with full API access, no credit card and no call. During early access, keys are issued by email rather than a self-serve dashboard.

What costs a credit?

One ID document scan is one credit. Adding face match is one more. Adding liveness is one more. A check with everything switched on is three credits, and you choose per verification which parts to run.

Should I use the API or the hosted flow?

Use the API if you already have capture screens or want full control of the experience. Use the hosted flow if you would rather send the user a link and let us handle the camera, the selfie and the retries.

Both run the same checks and return the same result format, so you can start with one and move to the other.

How do you handle ID images and personal data?

Images are processed by our verification pipeline, which includes cloud-based OCR sub-processors. You choose whether results are kept in a transaction vault with us for audit, or not stored and sent straight to your systems.

We do not sell verification data and do not use ID images for advertising. The privacy summary has the detail and is still marked as a draft.

What happens when a document is not recognized?

You get an explicit flag, not a silent pass. The response marks the document as unrecognized and your rules decide whether that becomes a review or a reject. Send us the document type and we will look at adding it.
