Questions.

Do I need a developer?+

No. We add the verification step to your Shopify checkout and configure it with you on a 15-minute call.

What does my customer actually do?+

At checkout they scan a government ID and take a quick selfie. Document, authenticity, and liveness checks run behind the scenes, and you get a pass or fail.

What happens when a check fails?+

Failed checks go to a simple manual review queue, so you decide what to do with the order.

What do I keep on file?+

A timestamped audit record for every verification — a record of each check — that you can pull anytime.

Who handles my customers' ID data?+

Verification runs on certified third-party infrastructure, and customer data is collected with the customer's explicit consent.

Is there a long contract?+

You start with a 60-day paid pilot, credited toward your annual plan. No minimums to start, and early-access pricing is locked for 12 months.
