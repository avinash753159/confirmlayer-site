Privacy

ConfirmLayer is an ID verification API. This page explains what data is involved when an ID is verified through our service, in plain language.

What we collect

When an end user verifies, they provide a photo of a government-issued ID and, optionally, a live selfie, with their explicit consent. We extract the document's data fields, check the document for signs of tampering, and compare the selfie to the ID photo. Our customers (the businesses using our API) also provide standard account and billing information.

How verification runs

Document images are processed by our verification pipeline, which includes cloud-based OCR sub-processors. We verify that the document is authentic and unaltered and that the presenter matches the ID photo. We do not query government or DMV databases.

Storage is your choice

Customers choose how verification data is handled: keep a transaction vault with us for audit and review, or disable storage and have results sent straight to their own systems. Retention settings are controlled by the customer, not by us.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell verification data.
  • We don't use ID images for advertising.
  • We don't share data with third parties except the sub-processors required to run verification.
